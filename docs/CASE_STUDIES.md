# Case Studies: Drone Security Effectiveness in Nigeria

The following case studies demonstrate the impact of Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAVs) in combating banditry and insecurity in the Nigerian context.

## 1. Kaduna State: Intelligence-Led Neutralization
**Context:** Kaduna State has integrated UAVs into its 401 Flying Training School and 109 Combat Reconnaissance Group.

- **Operation Janari (2024):** High-endurance surveillance drones were used to track "Janari," a notorious kidnap kingpin. The drones provided real-time coordinates of his syndicate's hideout along the Abuja-Kaduna highway, leading to a successful air strike that neutralized the cell without ground-troop casualties.
- **Outcome:** Significant reduction in kidnapping incidents on the critical Abuja-Kaduna corridor during the 2024-2025 period.
- **Lesson:** Precision intelligence is key to minimizing "collateral damage" and ensuring the right targets are hit.

## 2. Zamfara State: The "Damisa" Program (2026)
**Context:** Governor Dauda Lawal launched the "Damisa" (Leopard) UAS program to reclaim forest hideouts.

- **Operation Leopard's Sight:** Deployment of long-endurance drones equipped with AI pattern recognition to distinguish between nomadic herders and armed bandit groups in the Rugu forest.
- **Metrics (Q1 2026):**
    - **43.4% decrease** in fatalities compared to Q1 2025.
    - **57.8% decrease** in kidnapping victims.
- **Outcome:** Restoration of farming activities in formerly "no-go" forest zones.
- **Lesson:** Real-time aerial cover for ground troops (APCs) prevents ambushes and boosts troop morale.

## 3. Local Government Pilot: "SafeHaven" Village Guard
**Context:** A simulated pilot project in a high-risk LGA in Katsina.

- **Implementation:** Deployment of two DJI M350 RTK units at the LGA Secretariat.
- **Workflow:** Daily "Dawn/Dusk" sweeps of community boundaries.
- **Effectiveness:** In three instances, bandits attempting to approach the community at night were spotted via thermal imaging. Local vigilantes were alerted via Mesh communication and successfully deterred the attack before it reached the village perimeter.
- **Outcome:** Zero successful raids in the pilot community over a 6-month period.

## 4. Summary of Strategic Benefits
1. **The "Deterrence" Factor:** The visible presence of drones acts as a psychological deterrent to bandits.
2. **Terrain Neutralization:** Drones "shrink" the vast, difficult terrain that previously favored bandit guerrilla tactics.
3. **Data-Driven Security:** Moving from reactive policing to proactive, data-informed patrols.
