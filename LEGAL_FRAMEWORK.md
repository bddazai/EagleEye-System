### 1. Regulatory Compliance (NCAA & ONSA)
The operation of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in Nigeria is strictly regulated. 
- **User Responsibility:** The end-user (LGA, Agency, or Private Entity) is solely responsible for obtaining a **Remotely Piloted Aircraft System (RPAS) Operator's Certificate (ROC)** from the **Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA)**.
- **Security Clearance:** Use of this system requires explicit clearance from the **Office of the National Security Adviser (ONSA)**.
- **No Authorization:** This repository provides *how* to build the system, but does not provide the *legal right* to fly it.

### 2. Data Privacy & NDPR Compliance
Surveillance involves the processing of personal data, which is governed by the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)**.
- **Data Controller Status:** The entity deploying the system is the "Data Controller." Project contributors have no access to, control over, or responsibility for the data captured.
- **Privacy by Design:** Users are mandated to implement the privacy safeguards outlined in `docs/PRD.md`, including data encryption and restricted access logs.

### 3. Non-Weaponization & Ethical Use
EagleEye is designed for **peaceful community protection**.
- **Prohibited Use:** Any modification of the UAVs or software for offensive kinetic use (mounting weapons, explosives, or projectiles) is strictly prohibited under this framework.
- **Targeted Surveillance:** Use of this system for the unauthorized surveillance of individuals, political opponents, or private property without a legal warrant is a violation of ethical use.
